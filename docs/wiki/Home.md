# JobWarden

**The keeper of your job hunt.** Save job postings from your browser in one click, and keep every application's story,
from the first save to the offer. Self-hosted: it runs on your own computer, with your data in your own PostgreSQL, in
8 languages. Its AI features run on the assistant you already have (Claude Code or Codex), on your own plan: JobWarden
needs no API key and sends nothing anywhere.
