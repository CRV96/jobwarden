# Getting started

Once JobWarden runs ([Installation](Installation)), five minutes make the rest work. The app's **setup guide** walks
you through them: it opens on its own while a step is left (until you tick **Don't show this again**), and **Set up
JobWarden** in the profile menu brings it back until every step is done. It ticks each step as you do it, and says what
each turns on: the CV, your name and contact details (filled in right in the guide), the AI assistant, the browser
extension, the mailbox and the watchlist.

## 1. Create your profile

The first page asks for a name: that's your **profile**. It keeps its own jobs, CV, searches, inbox and settings, so
several people, or several job hunts, can share one JobWarden. Give it a password in **Settings → Your profile** if
others use the same browser. More on [Profiles and settings](Profiles-and-settings).

## 2. Save your first job

Either:

- **With the [browser extension](Browser-extension)** (the quickest, from then on), from the
  [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/bjklifdmffkhiffbicofeohgdcpmobek) or
  [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/jobwarden/): on a job posting, click its icon, then
  **Bookmark** or **I applied**. It reads the title, company, place, salary and description for you.
- **By hand:** **Jobs → Add job**, with the posting's link and what you know.
- **From a spreadsheet** you kept so far: **Settings → Your jobs → Import** ([how](Tracking-jobs#export-and-import)).

Each job gets a **timeline**: saved, applied, each interview, the offer or the rejection. Change its status from its side
panel, or switch the **Jobs** page to **Board** and drag its card to another column. Interviews, deadlines and days to
follow up show on the **Calendar**, and can remind you.

## 3. Add your CV

On the **CV** page, upload your CV (PDF, Word or text), or fill in the fields and let JobWarden build it. Everything the
assistant writes for you starts from it: tailored CVs, cover letters, messages, interview prep.

## 4. Connect your AI assistant (optional)

The AI features (finding jobs for your CV, scoring how well you fit, tailored CVs, cover letters, interview and
negotiation briefs...) are done by your own assistant: Claude Code or Codex, on your own plan. **Settings → AI
Assistant** gives the one command that connects it. See [AI assistant](AI-assistant).

## 5. Connect your mailbox (optional)

On the **Inbox** page, connect your mailbox with an app password: JobWarden reads recruiters' emails and offers each
rejection, interview or offer as an update of its job. Nothing changes a job without you, unless you say so in
**Settings → Inbox**. See [Inbox](Inbox).

## Then

- The **Home** page says what waits: jobs to review, emails, follow-ups due, the next interviews.
- Every page has a **How it works** button by its title.
- Missing something? **Suggest a feature**, in the profile menu.
